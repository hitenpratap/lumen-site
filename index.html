The real app

Five screens. One calm inbox.

Lumen running on a Mac with a local model and a demo inbox. The mail is made up; everything else is the app as it is.

Today

Your morning in 60 seconds

The first open of the day starts with a short brief your AI writes from the facts in your mail: what needs you, what is due, and how Lumen sorted the rest.

→How Lumen sorted your night, by count →Inbox forecast: need you, read later, handled →Ask about your mail in plain words
Inbox

Catch me up on any thread

Open a thread and Lumen summarises it, pulls out the to-dos and offers the next step, like adding the new meeting time to your calendar.

→Priority and Others tabs, with labels →One-tap replies in your own voice →Every AI line says which model wrote it
Shield

Phishing held back, with reasons

Suspicious mail never reaches your inbox. Lumen says why in plain words, and every check runs on your computer, without AI and offline.

→Links whose text lies about where they go →Pressure tactics and raw IP addresses →Delete and block, report, or release
Codes

Sign-in codes that clean up after themselves

One-time codes are pulled out of your mail and ready to copy, with a countdown to when they disappear.

→Masked until you reveal or copy →Hidden after 10 minutes, deleted after 24 hours →Never in search or lock-screen alerts
Your AI

Pick the AI for each job

Every feature shows where your email text goes. Run everything on Ollama or LM Studio, or send one feature to your own cloud key.

→Labels, spam, phishing and codes need no AI →Private numbers masked before any cloud call →Every request listed in the network log
Features

AI in every part of the inbox, never hidden.

A sorted inbox

Priority and Others, Smart Stacks and labels from a model that runs on your computer. Move a thread once and Lumen learns that sender.

A composer that drafts

Pick what you want to say and the tone; the draft rewrites live. Before you send, it catches a missing attachment or a promised time.

Search that answers

Gmail-style filters, or ask in plain words: "How much has Marco invoiced me this year?" gets a total, a chart and the emails behind it.

Translate in place

An email in another language gets a Translate button that swaps the words and keeps the email's layout. See original swaps it back.

Rules in plain words

"Never send emails from my lawyer to a cloud AI." Teach Lumen in a sentence, and it suggests rules from what you already do.

Sensitive info lock

Emails with ID, card or bank numbers can sit behind your system password or fingerprint.
