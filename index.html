Email that feels calm, not urgent.

Features

AI in every part of the inbox, never hidden.

Every line the AI wrote is marked, with the model that wrote it and where it ran.

A sorted inbox

Priority and Others tabs, Smart Stacks and labels from a model that runs on your computer. Move a thread once and Lumen learns that sender.

A morning brief

Your morning in 60 seconds: what needs you today, meetings and deliveries found in your mail, and what was handled overnight.

Catch me up

A summary of any thread with its to-dos, plus one-tap replies in your own voice. Emails in other languages translate in place, layout intact.

A composer that drafts

Pick what you want to say and the tone; the draft rewrites live. Checks before you send catch a missing attachment or a promised time.

Search that answers

Gmail-style filters, or ask in plain words: "How much has Marco invoiced me this year?" gets a total, a chart and the emails behind it.

Shield

Phishing held in quarantine with plain reasons, a code vault that hides sign-in codes, and a lock for emails with ID or bank numbers. Works offline, without AI.

Today

Your morning in 60 seconds

The first open of the day starts with a short brief: how many emails need you, what came in overnight, and how Lumen sorted the rest. Ask about your mail in plain words right from there.

Shield

Phishing stopped, with reasons

Suspicious mail is held back before it reaches your inbox. Lumen says why in plain words: a link that lies about where it goes, pressure tactics, a sender you have never heard from. Every check runs on your computer.

Codes

Sign-in codes, kept short-lived

One-time codes are pulled out of your mail and ready to copy. They stay masked until you reveal them, hide after 10 minutes and are deleted after 24 hours. They never show in search or notifications.

Privacy

Local only, by design.

No mail on our servers, ever. Lumen talks to your own email providers and, only if you choose, your own AI provider.

Stays on your computer

  • Every email and attachment
  • Contacts and the search index
  • AI summaries, drafts and labels
  • Your rules and writing style
  • Passwords and API keys, in the system keychain

What Lumen's server gets

  • Your name, email and country
  • Your OS and app version
  • Your notification choices and a device ID
  • No password. That's all, used for updates, security patches and notices.

Anonymous usage counts can be turned off at any time, and start off in the EU and UK.

Bring your own AI

Your model, your key, your choice.

Lumen ships no model and never sees your prompts. Run one on your computer, or use your own API key, and pick per feature and per account.
